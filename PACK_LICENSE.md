This license covers packs published by raycollabs: the free starter pack, paid packs and update feeds. It does not cover the Durai shell (Apache-2.0, see LICENSE).

## Paid packs and feeds
A paid pack is licensed to the organization named in the license token, for the seats and the dates in the token.
- You may install and use the pack in your own systems.
- You may keep using an installed pack after the token ends. Your running systems keep working. You receive no new updates.
- You may not copy, share, resell or publish the pack or the feed.
- You may not use the pack to train a model that you sell as a competing pack.
