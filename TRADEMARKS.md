# Trademark policy (draft for legal review)

"Durai" and the Durai logo are marks of raycollabs.

You may:
- State that your product works with Durai or is based on the Durai shell.
- Use the name in plain text to refer to the project.

You may not:
- Name a fork or a product "Durai", or a name that is close to it.
- Claim that a pack you wrote is signed, certified or supported by raycollabs.
- Use the marks to suggest that raycollabs endorses you.

A fork must use a different name and remove the logo.
This text is a draft. It is not legal advice. A lawyer must review it before release.
